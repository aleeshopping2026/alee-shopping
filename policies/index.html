PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alee Shopping (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit www.aleeshopping.com, create an account, shop, sell, post, or message through our marketplace. It applies to the website and related features.

1. Information we collect

Account and sign-in details. Depending on how you sign in, we process your email address, phone number, account identifier, display name, and profile image. Email/password and phone verification are handled through Supabase Auth. We do not receive your Google password.

Google sign-in information. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares the basic account details you authorize, such as your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile image. We use these details to create or access your Alee Shopping account, identify your profile, and keep your sign-in secure.

Orders and shop information. When you place or manage an order, we process details such as your name, delivery address, phone numbers, order items, selected options, amount, payment method, and order notes/status. Seller accounts may provide shop names, contact details, logos, listings, and other profile information.

Content and messages. We process product listings, shop profiles, feed posts, comments, reviews, uploaded photos, messages, and attachments that you choose to submit or send.

Use of the site and technical details. We may record searches, product views, favorites, and interactions to provide search, recommendations, and marketplace features. Your browser also sends basic technical information, such as device/browser details and IP address, to the website and service providers. We request approximate country information from IPAPI to select a country and currency.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; operate the marketplace; show profiles and listings; receive and manage orders; connect buyers and sellers; provide messaging and support; remember cart, checkout, and preference settings; improve search and recommendations; deliver notifications when you opt in; prevent misuse; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Google account information is used for sign-in, account management, and displaying your account profile. We do not sell Google user data or use it to target advertising. We do not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to the features you choose.

3. Who can see information

Relevant shops. When you place an order, the shop receiving it can see the customer and order details needed to review and fulfil that order, including your name, contact number, delivery address, items, and notes.

Other users. Shop profiles, listings, and feed posts are public. Comments and reviews may be visible with the related post or product. Messages are available to the conversation participants and are stored to provide the messaging service.

Service providers. We use Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and real-time features; Google when you choose Google sign-in; OneSignal if you enable browser notifications; and IPAPI for approximate country detection. These providers process information to provide their services under their own privacy terms. If you choose to open WhatsApp or another external link, that service’s privacy policy applies.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where needed to protect users or the service, or where disclosure is required by law.

4. Public posts and private messages

Information you add to a public shop profile, product listing, feed post, public comment, or review can be viewed by other visitors. Do not include private contact, financial, or identity information in public content. Messages and attachments are shared with the people in that conversation; use care when sending personal information.

5. Browser storage

The site uses browser storage, including local storage and session storage, to remember your cart, checkout details, favorites, recent searches or views, theme, and sign-in/session state. Checkout details saved this way remain in that browser on your device until you clear them or remove them through the site. Anyone with access to your device or browser profile may be able to see locally saved information. You can clear browser storage in your browser settings, though some site features may then forget saved items or require you to sign in again. Google, authentication, hosting, or notification providers may also use their own cookies or similar technologies for security and service operation.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep account, order, message, and marketplace information for as long as needed to operate the service, resolve issues, maintain records, and protect the platform. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete account information. Some information may need to be retained for a limited time for security, backups, disputes, or legal recordkeeping. Removing locally saved checkout details from your browser does not delete an order already submitted to a shop.

7. Your choices

You can update profile information in your account, manage browser notification permission in your device or browser, and clear locally saved site data through the browser. To ask a question or request access, correction, or deletion of account information, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your account before acting on a request.

8. Security and processing locations

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Our service providers may process or store information in the countries where their systems operate, subject to their service terms and configurations.

9. Children

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through the site without your permission, contact us and we will review the request.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service or privacy practices change. The current version and its update date will be posted on this page.

11. Contact Alee Shopping

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at aleeshoppingmain@gmail.com or WhatsApp 076 138 9936. You can also visit www.aleeshopping.com.
